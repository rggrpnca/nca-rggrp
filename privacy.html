Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

The National Crime Agency Portal ("we", "our", "us") is operated within the Royal Grenadier Guards Roleplay community on Roblox. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our portal.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an application through our Academy Gateway, we collect the following information:

• Roblox username and profile link

• Discord username and ID

• Email address

• Age bracket

• Application responses and answers

• 2FA proof screenshots uploaded during the application process

When you authenticate via Roblox OAuth, we collect your Roblox user ID, username, and group membership information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the purpose of evaluating your application to the NCA Academy. Your information will not be shared with third parties outside of the Royal Grenadier Guards Roleplay community.

Application data is reviewed by authorised NCA administrators only and is stored securely in our database.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party database provider. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access.

Uploaded files such as 2FA proof screenshots are stored securely and are only accessible to authorised administrators.

5. Data Retention

Application data is retained for the duration of the recruitment wave. After each wave concludes, data may be cleared at the discretion of NCA administrators.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your application data at any time by contacting an NCA administrator through official channels in the Royal Grenadier Guards Roleplay community.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact an NCA administrator through the Royal Grenadier Guards Roleplay community.
